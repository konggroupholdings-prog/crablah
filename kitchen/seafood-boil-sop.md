# CrabLah kitchen SOP

Sri Lankan mud crab, finished in Singapore gravy, packed hot for home. Weigh live. Do not substitute two 500g stall crabs for one printed size.

## Two finish paths

| Path | Gravies | Method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A · milk-curry toss | Thai milk (pong karee) | Boil until done, drain hard, toss in evaporated-milk yellow curry and egg |
| B · wok finish | Salted egg, chilli, black pepper | Boil to 80%, drain, wok-coat, pack with extra gravy |

Seafood Bag (the menu): salted egg, chilli, black pepper only. Solo Lanka and Twin Lanka also offer Thai milk.

Do not boil salted egg or chilli gravy in the pot. Yolks split and chilli waters down.

## Court bouillon

Per 1650 Mix Bag (scale down):

- 8 L water
- 80 g seasoned boil salt
- 1 lemon, halved
- 1 onion, 1 head garlic smashed
- 4 bay leaves
- 20 g black peppercorn

Bring to a rolling boil. Potatoes should taste of the pot, not tap water.

## Load order

1. Baby potatoes — 10–12 min
2. Corn — 5–6 min (sausage only if they added it)
3. Crab, cleaned and cut — 8–10 min depending on size
4. Lobster (if ordered) — 7–8 min, split, same pot as crab
5. Clams + mussels — 3–4 min, discard any that stay shut
6. Prawns — 2–3 min, just opaque
7. Sotong — 60–90 seconds, just curled

Lift out with a spider. Shake water off. Wet seafood makes watery sauce.

## Crab prep

- Live until the order is paid.
- Kill humanely, lift the carapace, remove gills and guts, crack claws once, cut body into 4–6.
- Weigh **before** cooking. That is the size the customer paid for.
- Grades on the menu: Lanka 650 (600–700g) · 750 · 850 · Kilo (900g–1kg). No 500–600g.
- **Twin Lanka** = two of the same size, one wok, one $30 cook. Extra crabs after that are the add-on price.
- **650 Mix Bag** = Mix Bag + 1 × 650. **Mix Kilo Bag** = double seafood (400g each) + 1 × kilo. **1650 Mix Bag** = triple seafood (600g each) + kilo + 650.
- **Mix Bag** = no crab, no scallops, no sausage. Prawns, mussels, la la, sotong **200g each**, corn, potato, **6 mantou**. Extra Lanka tossed in the same wok if they add it.
- **Solo Lanka** and **Twin Lanka** include the first **6 fried mantou** FOC (cook already sends them). Extra mantou is sold in packs of 6.
- Add-on fried mantou is always **6 pcs**. Every Seafood Bag packs **6 mantou**.
- Add-on prawn, la la, mussels, sotong are all **200g**. Add-on Boston lobster is **1 pc** (buy $20, sell $32). Toss in the same gravy. No second cook.
- **Kilo** (Solo Kilo, Twin Kilo, Mix Kilo Bag, 1650 Mix Bag, extra kilo crab): order a day ahead, next-day delivery. Friday–Sunday: everything is next-day.
- Expect 25–40% weight loss after cleaning and cooking.

## Path A — butter toss

Warm the sauce (do not boil garlic butter until it splits).

For one 650 Mix Bag:

- 90 g unsalted butter
- 25 g minced garlic
- 12 g chilli-butter spice (or mala paste 40 g)
- 8 g sugar
- 10 g lemon juice
- pinch of msg or chicken powder

Toss drained seafood + corn + potato until coated. Pack in a deep tray with extra sauce tub. Add sausage only if they ordered it.

## Path B — wok finish

### Salted egg

Per 650 Mix Bag (scale 1.8× for Mix Kilo Bag, 2.6× for 1650 Mix Bag):

- 4 salted egg yolks, steamed and mashed
- 70 g butter
- 8 curry leaf stalks
- 4 chilli padi, smashed
- 40 ml evaporated milk
- 8 g sugar
- 4 g chicken powder
- optional 10 g milk powder for cling

Butter foam, curry leaf, chilli padi, yolks until fragrant and slightly sandy, splash milk, toss seafood on high heat 45–60 seconds. Sauce should coat, not soup.

### Chilli

Batch this gravy in the morning. It holds.

- 80 g chilli paste (rempah)
- 40 g tomato paste / ketchup mix
- 20 g garlic
- 15 g ginger
- 12 g sugar
- 8 g vinegar or lime
- 200 ml stock
- 1 egg white foamed in at the end for the classic pull (optional)

Wok the parcooked seafood in 250–300 ml gravy. Pack **extra 200 ml in a tub**.

Heat is on the slip. **Mild spicy** is this recipe. **More spicy** is the same gravy plus extra chilli padi (3–4 smashed) in the wok — still chilli crab, not mala.

### Black pepper

- 60 g butter
- 25 g crushed black pepper (not fine powder)
- 15 g garlic
- 15 ml oyster sauce
- 10 ml dark soya
- 10 ml Worcestershire
- 8 g sugar

High heat, fast toss. Pepper burns if it sits on the wok.

## Packing

Insulated bag, in this order:

1. Banana leaf or baking paper liner in a deep aluminium tray
2. Seafood in a tied leak-proof bag or lidded tray
3. Extra sauce tub, taped shut
4. Mantou in a vented box
5. Rice pack if ordered
6. Kit: gloves, wet wipes, crab crackers, wooden picks
7. Order slip with ID, flavour, slot, allergy note

Target: out the door within 12 minutes of plating.

## Food safety

- Hold above 60°C until handover. If a rider is late, reheat sauce separately, do not recook prawns.
- Shellfish from the same supplier batch. If clams smell of mud, do not send.
- Label: cooked on {time}, keep refrigerated if not eating within 2 hours, reheat until steaming.
