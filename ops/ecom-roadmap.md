# Ecom platforms — later, not launch

Day-one till is **website + PayNow**. Grab, Foodpanda, Shopee and Lazada take 25–35% and want a registered merchant. List them after UEN, hygiene cert, and a second price list.

## Commission reality

Rule: **platform price = direct price × 1.30**, rounded to .90.

| Direct | Grab / Foodpanda / Shopee |
| --- | --- |
| Lanka 650 $88 | $114.90 |
| Lanka 750 $98 | $127.90 |
| Lanka Kilo $118 | $153.90 |
| Lanka Double $148 | $192.90 |
| Mix Bag $52 | $67.90 |
| 650 Mix Bag $108 | $140.90 |
| Mix Kilo Bag $168 | $218.90 |
| 1650 Mix Bag $258 | $335.90 |

Never let platform pricing leak onto the website. Two catalogues, same SKU codes.

## GrabFood / Foodpanda

- Need: company, F&B licence / home-based rules as they stand, outlet address, packaging photos.
- Menu: fewer SKUs. Lanka 650 / 750 + Mix Bag / 650 Mix Bag + 3 gravies. Cut 1650 Mix Bag until you can hit the SLA.
- Prep time: 40 minutes. Do not promise 20.
- No PayNow on Grab. Their wallet is the till.

## Shopee / Lazada

These are not hot-food platforms. Use them for frozen kits and sauce tubs. Do not sell freshly cooked crab on Shopee.

Listing title pattern:

`[CrabLah] Salted Egg / Chilli / Black Pepper Sri Lankan Mud Crab 1kg Cooked Delivery Singapore`
